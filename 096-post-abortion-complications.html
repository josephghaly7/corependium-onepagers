Post Abortion Complications

OB/GYN · Week 42 · src: Post Bariatric Surgery Complications
Bottom line: Unsafe abortion complications—hemorrhage, sepsis, perforation—demand ED resuscitation and early OB/GYN surgical consultation.

⚖ Management Pathway

● assess/decide   ● act/treat   ● refer/transfer
1Assess vitals; perform thorough speculum and bimanual exam for any post-abortion bleeding.
↓
2If severe bleeding: obtain CBC, coagulation panel, type and screen; ultrasound if no laceration source.
↓
3Small laceration: direct pressure or silver nitrate; larger: OB repair with absorbable sutures.
↓
4Retained products or significant intrauterine blood: vacuum aspiration (OB usually to OR).
↓
5Uterine atony: fundal massage then misoprostol 800-1000 mcg per rectum; methylergonovine next.
↓
6Unstable severe bleeding: resuscitate, transfuse, give TXA; activate MTP after 2 units pRBCs.
↓
7Refractory bleeding or pseudoaneurysm: IR uterine artery embolization, or laparotomy/laparoscopy with hysterectomy last.
↓
8Suspected infection: sepsis labs, blood and cervical cultures, ultrasound for retained products.
↓
9Antibiotics: gentamicin/clindamycin/nafcillin or piperacillin-tazobactam/clindamycin; OB for source control D&C.
↓
10Suspect uterine/bowel/bladder perforation: ultrasound first; if negative or concern, CT; consult OB, urology, general surgery.

📝 Key Facts

  • Safe abortion major complication rate 0.1-0.16%; maternal mortality ≤0.62 per 100,000.
  • Unsafe abortion: ~7 million complications yearly, 68,000 maternal deaths; mortality 30-220 per 100,000.
  • Unsafe vs safe abortion maternal death: 50-360 fold increased risk.
  • Complication rate: one in three and a half unsafe abortions vs one in a thousand safe.
  • Unsafe abortion bleeding: severe up to 3%, non-severe ~44%; safe abortion significant bleeding <2%.
  • Unsafe abortion infection: severe 1 in 20, non-severe 1 in 4; safe abortion infection <0.2%.
  • Uterine perforation occurs in 0.1-2% of safe abortions; higher in unsafe.
  • Foreign body insertion complications severe in ~7% of unsafe abortions.
  • Misoprostol toxic dose 3-8 mg (severe GI, fever, rhabdomyolysis, altered); 12 mg causes multi-organ failure/death.

Attending Pearls

  • Post-abortion bleeding workup mirrors postpartum hemorrhage: tone, tissue, trauma, thrombin—the four T's.
  • Medical abortion bleeding should taper after 3-8 hours; heavier bleeding after 8-10 hours signals complication.
  • Unsafe abortion infections are polymicrobial; group A strep and clostridia can cause rapid toxic shock.
  • In post-abortion sepsis, prioritize source control (D&C with vacuum aspiration) and involve OB early.

Pitfalls

  • Assuming heavy bleeding after 8-10 hours post medical abortion is normal; it signals complication.
  • Missing post-abortion infection as the sepsis source in young women with fever, hypotension, abdominal pain.
  • Relying on negative ultrasound to exclude bowel or bladder perforation; get CT if concerned.
  • Forgetting to involve general surgery when OB is unavailable for unstable bleeding.
