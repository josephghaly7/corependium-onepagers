Post Abortion Complications

OB/GYN · Week 42 · 19 min
Bottom line: Unsafe abortion complications—bleeding, infection, trauma—require ED resuscitation and early OB.

⚖ Management Pathway

● assess/decide   ● act/treat   ● refer/transfer
1Assess vital signs and hemodynamics; perform thorough speculum and bimanual exam for bleeding after abortion.
↓
2If severe bleeding, get CBC, coagulation panel, type/screen; if no source, ultrasound.
↓
3If laceration: apply direct pressure or silver nitrate for small; OB repairs larger with absorbable sutures.
↓
4If retained products or significant intrauterine blood, arrange vacuum aspiration; OB usually takes to OR.
↓
5If uterine atony, give fundal massage and misoprostol 800-1000 mcg PR; add methylergonovine if needed.
↓
6If unstable severe bleeding, resuscitate, transfuse, give TXA; activate massive transfusion protocol after 2 units pRBCs.
↓
7If refractory bleeding, consider IR uterine artery embolization or surgical laparotomy/laparoscopy with hysterectomy as last resort.
↓
8For suspected infection, obtain sepsis labs, blood and cervical cultures, and ultrasound looking for retained products.
↓
9Give broad-spectrum antibiotics (gentamicin/clindamycin/nafcillin or pip-tazo/clindamycin); consult OB for source control.
↓
10If suspected uterine/bowel/bladder perforation, start with ultrasound; if negative or concern, CT and consult OB/general surgery/urology.

💊 Doses

Misoprostol800-1000 mcg PRFirst-line uterotonic for uterine atony/bleeding after abortion.

📝 Key Facts

  • Safe abortion major complication rate 0.1-0.16%; maternal mortality ≤0.62 per 100,000.
  • Unsafe abortion: ~7 million complications yearly, 68,000 maternal deaths; mortality 30-220 per 100,000.
  • Medical abortion bleeding starts ~2 hours, peaks 3-8 hours, lasts median 11-13 days; excessive = 2 pads/hour for 2 hours.
  • Unsafe abortion: severe bleeding up to 3%, non-severe 44%; severe infection 1 in 20, non-severe 1 in 4.
  • Misoprostol toxic dose 3-8 mg; 12 mg causes multi-organ failure/death; absorbed in 90 minutes.
  • Uterine perforation occurs in 0.1-2% of safe abortions; higher in unsafe; small perforations may go undetected.

Attending Pearls

  • Post-abortion bleeding workup mirrors postpartum hemorrhage: think tone, tissue, trauma, thrombin.
  • Medical abortion bleeding should taper after 3-8 hours; heavier bleeding after 8-10 hours signals complication.
  • Unsafe abortion infections are polymicrobial; group A strep and clostridia can cause rapid toxic shock.
  • In post-abortion sepsis, prioritize source control (D&C with vacuum aspiration) and involve OB early.

Pitfalls

  • Assuming heavy bleeding after 8-10 hours is normal after medical abortion; it signals a complication.
  • Forgetting to involve general surgery when OB is not in-house for unstable bleeding.
  • Missing post-abortion infection as the source of sepsis in young women with fever, hypotension, abdominal pain.
  • Relying on a negative ultrasound to exclude bowel or bladder perforation; get CT if concerned.
