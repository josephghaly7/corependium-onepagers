Airway Basics: Preoxygenation, Paralysis, and Placement

Pulmonary · Week 8 · src: Airway Management
Bottom line: Preoxygenation prolongs safe apnea time; NRB plus apneic O2 and DSI save difficult airways.

⚖ Management Pathway

● assess/decide   ● act/treat   ● refer/transfer
1Position upright, or reverse Trendelenburg 20-30 degrees if spinal immobilization.
↓
2Awake/cooperative: tight non-rebreather cranked past 15 L/min; 3 minutes or 8 deep breaths.
↓
3Place nasal cannula under mask; after sedation crank nasal cannula to 15-30 L/min for apneic oxygenation.
↓
4Unconscious breathing: NRB plus nasal cannula, or assist with BVM; give big breaths before paralyzing.
↓
5Apneic/desaturating: BVM two-person, lift chin into bag, match rate, avoid overbagging.
↓
6Severely hypoxemic despite high-flow O2: DSI with ketamine to mellow, then CPAP/BiPAP to 100%.
↓
7Proceed to intubation; positive pressure in DSI raises aspiration risk.
↓
8Avoid bagging if sats good; bagging risks aspiration and defeats RSI.

📝 Key Facts

  • Preoxygenation goals: SpO2 100%, denitrogenate lungs, oxygenate bloodstream.
  • Lungs/alveoli make up 95% of oxygen reservoir; bloodstream only 5%.
  • Room air is 20-21% oxygen; non-rebreather at 15 L/min gives 60-70% FiO2; cranked to 30-60 L/min gives >90%, not 100%.
  • Head elevation improves oxygenation and ventilation and decreases posterior lung atelectasis.
  • Apneic oxygenation: passive gas exchange without ventilation using high-flow nasal cannula or BVM with PEEP valve and good seal.
  • DSI: ketamine mellow without stopping breathing, then noninvasive ventilation (CPAP/BiPAP) to reach 100% in severely hypoxemic patients.

Attending Pearls

  • Preoxygenation is everything; if you preoxygenate well, everything else gets easier.
  • Lift the chin up into the bag; don't smush the bag down onto the patient's face.
  • Use two-person bagging; hands tire, spasm, and get sweaty in stressful situations.
  • Apneic oxygenation buys minutes of 100% sat and is a lifesaver in difficult airways.

Pitfalls

  • Don't assume non-rebreather gives 100% FiO2; at 15 L/min it's only 60-70%.
  • Don't hold BVM near face without seal; must hold seal and assist ventilations.
  • Don't crank nasal cannula before induction; high flow is uncomfortable.
  • Don't overbag or bag patients with good sats; insufflates stomach and increases aspiration risk.
  • Avoid bagging if sats good; aspiration risk defeats RSI purpose.
